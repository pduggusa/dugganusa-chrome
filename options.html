When ON, the extension reads the visible text of every page you visit, extracts up to 30 IPs, domains, SHA256 hashes and CVEs, and sends those values to analytics.dugganusa.com to be checked against the threat feed. That includes internal hostnames, private IPs and staging domains rendered on intranet, HR, banking and healthcare pages. Leave this OFF unless you understand and accept that.

With it OFF, nothing is read or transmitted automatically. You can still scan on demand: click Scan this page in the toolbar popup, or right-click selected text and choose the DugganUSA lookup.
